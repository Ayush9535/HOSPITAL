## 1. 🏥 Hospital Admin

> **Who is this?** The Hospital Owner or Administrator — the person who oversees everything.

The Hospital Admin has the **highest level of access** inside the hospital account. They can see, manage, and configure everything.

---

### 📊 Dashboard — At a Glance View

When the admin logs in, they see a **live overview** of the entire hospital:

- Total number of patients registered today
- Number of OPD appointments for the day
- Number of IPD (admitted) patients
- Total revenue collected today
- Number of active doctors
- Pending support tickets
- Recent activity feed

---

### 🧑 Patient Management

The admin can fully manage all patient records:

- ✅ Register new patients (name, age, gender, contact, address, blood group)
- ✅ View complete list of all patients
- ✅ Search patients by name, phone, or patient ID
- ✅ View a patient's full history — past OPD visits, IPD admissions, prescriptions, and billing
- ✅ Edit patient details when needed

---

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Management

The admin manages all doctors in the hospital:

- ✅ Add new doctors with their details (name, specialization, qualification, experience)
- ✅ Set doctor availability / schedule
- ✅ Set consultation fees for each doctor
- ✅ View all doctors and their profiles
- ✅ Create login credentials for each doctor so they can access their own dashboard
- ✅ Deactivate a doctor's account if they leave the hospital

---

### 🧑‍💼 Staff Management

The admin can manage support staff:

- ✅ Add Receptionists — create their accounts and assign them to the front desk
- ✅ Add Pharmacists — create their accounts for pharmacy access
- ✅ View all staff members
- ✅ Deactivate staff accounts when needed

---

### 📅 OPD (Out-Patient Department) Management

- ✅ View all OPD appointments for the day / week / month
- ✅ View patient queue status in real time
- ✅ See which patients are waiting, in consultation, or done
- ✅ View appointment history for any patient
- ✅ Filter appointments by doctor or date

---

### 🛏️ IPD (In-Patient Department) Management

For patients who are admitted to the hospital:

- ✅ View all currently admitted patients
- ✅ See which ward and bed each patient is in
- ✅ View admission details — date, diagnosis, assigned doctor
- ✅ View and manage ward & bed availability
- ✅ Track discharge summaries
- ✅ See IPD billing for admitted patients

---

### 🏨 Ward & Bed Management

- ✅ Create and manage wards (e.g., General Ward, ICU, Maternity Ward)
- ✅ Add beds to each ward
- ✅ See which beds are occupied, available, or under maintenance
- ✅ Assign beds to admitted patients

---

### 💰 Billing & Payment Management

Complete control over patient billing:

- ✅ View all billing records — OPD and IPD
- ✅ Create bills with multiple line items (consultation fee, lab charges, medicine charges, etc.)
- ✅ Add medicine charges directly to a patient's bill
- ✅ Record partial payments and track outstanding balances
- ✅ Mark bills as Paid / Partially Paid / Unpaid
- ✅ View payment history for any patient
- ✅ Filter bills by date, patient, or payment status

---

### 💊 Medicine & Inventory Access

The admin has full view and control:

- ✅ View the pharmacy's current medicine stock
- ✅ Add medicines to the hospital's medicine list
- ✅ Configure medicine fees that appear on patient bills

---

### 📊 Reports & Analytics

The admin can generate detailed reports:

- ✅ **Revenue Report** — total income by day, week, or month
- ✅ **OPD Report** — number of patients, doctor-wise breakdown
- ✅ **IPD Report** — admissions, discharges, occupancy
- ✅ **Pharmacy Sales Report** — medicine sales and revenue
- ✅ **Outstanding Payments Report** — unpaid or partially paid bills
- ✅ **Stock Report** — current medicine inventory levels
- ✅ **Expiry Report** — medicines nearing or past expiry date

---

### ⚙️ Hospital Settings & Configuration

- ✅ Update hospital name, address, contact details, and logo
- ✅ Configure the hospital's consultation fee types
- ✅ Set up custom fee categories
- ✅ Manage hospital-specific preferences

---

### 🎫 Support & Help

- ✅ Raise support tickets to contact the platform team
- ✅ View status of all raised tickets (Open / In Progress / Resolved)
- ✅ View FAQ / Help articles

---

### 🔍 Audit Log

- ✅ View a complete log of all actions performed in the system (who did what and when)
- ✅ Useful for internal accountability and tracking

---

## 2. 👨‍⚕️ Doctor

> **Who is this?** The practicing physician or specialist who consults patients.

The Doctor gets their own private dashboard. They can only see their own patients and appointments — not other doctors' data.

---

### 📅 Appointments

- ✅ View today's appointment list
- ✅ See patient details before the consultation starts
- ✅ View upcoming appointments by date
- ✅ See appointment status (Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled)

---

### 🧑 Patient Consultation

- ✅ View patient's basic information and medical history
- ✅ Record consultation notes / diagnosis
- ✅ Write prescriptions — add medicines with dosage, frequency, and duration
- ✅ View past prescriptions for the same patient
- ✅ Add lab test recommendations

---

### 📋 OPD Records

- ✅ View all past OPD records for patients they have treated
- ✅ Search patients by name or ID
- ✅ View complete visit history for any patient

---

### 🛏️ IPD Patients

- ✅ View their currently admitted (IPD) patients
- ✅ See patient condition, ward, and bed details
- ✅ Add daily notes and treatment updates for admitted patients
- ✅ Write discharge summaries when a patient is ready to go home

---

### 📊 Personal Stats

- ✅ View total patients seen today
- ✅ View weekly and monthly consultation counts

---

---

## 3. 🧑‍💼 Receptionist

> **Who is this?** The front desk staff who manages patient flow and appointments.

The Receptionist is the **first point of contact** for patients. They handle registration and scheduling.

---

### 🧑 Patient Registration

- ✅ Register new patients quickly at the front desk
- ✅ Search existing patients by name or phone number
- ✅ View and update patient contact details

---

### 📅 Appointment Booking

- ✅ Book new appointments for patients
- ✅ Select doctor, date, and time slot
- ✅ Cancel or reschedule existing appointments
- ✅ View the full appointment list for the day
- ✅ Filter appointments by doctor

---

### 🔢 OPD Queue Management

- ✅ View the live patient queue for each doctor
- ✅ See which patient's turn is currently active
- ✅ Mark patients as arrived, in-consultation, or done
- ✅ Add patients to the walk-in queue directly

---

### 🛏️ IPD Admission

- ✅ Admit a patient for inpatient care
- ✅ Assign ward and bed during admission
- ✅ Record the admitting doctor and reason for admission
- ✅ View all currently admitted patients

---

### 💰 Billing Assistance

- ✅ View patient bills
- ✅ Record payments received at the front desk
- ✅ Check outstanding balances for a patient
- ✅ Generate and view billing summaries

---

### 📋 Daily Summary View

- ✅ See today's total appointments
- ✅ See how many patients are waiting vs. done
- ✅ View new patient registrations for the day

---

---

## 4. 💊 Pharmacy / Pharmacist

> **Who is this?** The pharmacy staff who manages medicines, stock, and patient medicine dispensing.

The Pharmacist has a dedicated pharmacy dashboard with full control over medicines, stock, and sales.

---

### 📦 Medicine Master (Medicine Database)

- ✅ Add medicines to the system with full details (name, composition, category, manufacturer)
- ✅ View and search the complete medicine list
- ✅ Set pricing, HSN code, and GST details for each medicine
- ✅ Edit or update medicine information

---

### 🏷️ Categories & Manufacturers

- ✅ Create and manage medicine categories (e.g., Antibiotics, Vitamins, Syrups)
- ✅ Add and manage manufacturer records
- ✅ Add and manage supplier details for purchasing

---

### 📥 Purchase / Stock-In

- ✅ Record medicine purchases from suppliers
- ✅ Add batch number, manufacturing date, and expiry date for every purchase
- ✅ Automatically update stock when a purchase is recorded
- ✅ View full purchase history
- ✅ Filter purchases by supplier or date

---

### 📊 Inventory / Stock Management

- ✅ View current stock levels for all medicines
- ✅ See stock quantity, batch details, and expiry dates
- ✅ Get alerts for low stock medicines
- ✅ Filter and search stock by medicine name or category

---

### ⚠️ Expiry Management

- ✅ View all medicines that are **expiring soon** (within 30, 60, or 90 days)
- ✅ View medicines that have **already expired**
- ✅ Take action to remove expired stock

---

### 🛒 Billing Counter (Medicine Sales)

- ✅ Create medicine sale bills for patients (walk-in or OPD/IPD patients)
- ✅ Search and add medicines to the bill easily
- ✅ Auto-calculate quantities, prices, and GST
- ✅ Apply discounts if needed
- ✅ Generate a final bill/invoice for the patient
- ✅ Automatically reduce stock when a sale is made
- ✅ Record payment (Cash / Card / UPI)

---

### 📋 Prescription View

- ✅ View prescriptions written by doctors for patients
- ✅ Directly dispense medicines from a prescription
- ✅ Mark prescriptions as dispensed once medicines are given

---

### 🔄 Returns Management

- ✅ Record medicine returns from patients
- ✅ Process purchase returns back to suppliers
- ✅ Adjust stock automatically on returns

---

### 📊 Pharmacy Reports

- ✅ **Sales Report** — total medicines sold by date or medicine name
- ✅ **Purchase Report** — all stock purchases within a date range
- ✅ **Stock Report** — current inventory snapshot
- ✅ **Expiry Report** — medicines nearing or past expiry
- ✅ **Revenue Summary** — total pharmacy earnings

---
